# Business concept: overview

## Core idea

Build an EdTech platform for primary schools that combines:

- formative pre-assessment
- identification of misconceptions and gaps
- knowledge of the lesson or unit learning objective
- pupil context such as SEND/EAL and teacher observations
- adaptive teaching recommendations
- generation of appropriate classroom resources and scaffolds

The goal is not to create different curricula for different pupils.

The goal is:

> Every child works towards the same ambitious learning objective, while the platform helps the teacher identify and apply the right adaptations for each pupil.

## The problem

Teachers often need to work out:

1. What does each pupil already know?
2. What prerequisite knowledge is missing?
3. What misconception is causing an error?
4. Which pupils need additional support?
5. Which pupils are already secure?
6. What adaptation should be made during the lesson?
7. What resources or scaffolds should be provided?
8. When should those scaffolds be removed?

A lot of this is currently based on teacher judgement, marking, spreadsheets and manual planning.

Existing products tend to solve only part of the problem.

## Existing market

### Adaptive learning platforms

Products such as:

- CENTURY
- Sumdog
- IXL
- Sparx Maths
- Eedi

already assess pupils and personalise learning.

Typical flow:

```text
Child
  ↓
Diagnostic assessment
  ↓
Identify gaps
  ↓
Recommended digital learning
  ↓
Child completes work inside the platform
```

### Worksheet / resource generators

Products such as:

- ClassMind
- WorksheetForge
- Schoolgen

can generate worksheets and differentiated resources.

Typical flow:

```text
Teacher
  ↓
Learning objective
  ↓
Choose support / core / challenge
  ↓
Worksheet generated
```

The major gap is joining these two workflows together.

## Product opportunity

A possible workflow:

```text
Today's learning objective
          ↓
Short diagnostic
          ↓
Identify prior knowledge
and misconceptions
          ↓
Combine with pupil context
          ↓
Recommend adaptations
          ↓
Teacher reviews / approves
          ↓
Teach whole-class lesson
          ↓
Provide targeted support,
scaffolds and challenge
          ↓
Check understanding again
```

The key loop is:

> Assess → Diagnose → Recommend adaptation → Teach → Check → Adapt again
