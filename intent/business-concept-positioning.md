# Business concept: positioning

## What the teacher might see

Before a lesson:

```text
LO: Add two 2-digit numbers crossing ten

Diagnostic complete: 28 / 30 pupils

Class findings
──────────────────────────────────
17 pupils ready for planned lesson

5 pupils:
Need additional support with exchanging

3 pupils:
Number bonds are insecure

3 pupils:
Already secure with the objective
```

Then:

```text
Suggested adaptive teaching plan

Whole-class modelling
──────────────────────────────────
Explicitly model exchanging using
place-value counters.

Pupils needing exchanging support
──────────────────────────────────
Provide counters during independent work.
Scaffold Q1–Q2.
Review after Q2.

Pupils with insecure number bonds
──────────────────────────────────
Provide number-bond prompt.
Teacher check-in before Q1.

Secure pupils
──────────────────────────────────
Complete core task.
Move to reasoning after Q4 if secure.
```

That is potentially much more useful than simply generating three worksheets.

## Core proposition

A concise proposition could be:

> Every child works towards the same ambitious learning objective. The platform identifies what each child already understands and gives the teacher the right adaptations to help them get there.

Alternative:

> Adaptive teaching powered by formative assessment.

Or:

> Know what every pupil needs before you start teaching.

## Business positioning

The product is not primarily:

- a lesson-plan generator
- a worksheet generator
- an online tutoring platform
- an adaptive learning game
- an MIS

Instead it sits between:

```text
Curriculum
    +
Assessment
    +
MIS / pupil context
    +
Teacher knowledge
        ↓
Adaptive teaching engine
        ↓
Teacher action
```

A particularly strong positioning idea is:

> Adaptive learning for the physical classroom.

Many existing adaptive-learning products keep the pupil inside their digital platform.

This product would use technology to decide **how to teach**, then allow the pupil to continue learning through normal classroom teaching, books, worksheets and manipulatives.

## Potential competitive advantage

Existing products generally fall into one of two camps.

### Adaptive platforms

```text
Assessment
   ↓
Gap identified
   ↓
Child gets personalised digital content
```

### AI planning/resource platforms

```text
Learning objective
   ↓
Teacher selects difficulty
   ↓
AI generates resource
```

The opportunity is:

```text
Learning objective
       +
Diagnostic assessment
       +
Historic pupil knowledge
       +
SEND / EAL / classroom context
       ↓
Misconception analysis
       ↓
Adaptive teaching recommendation
       ↓
Classroom resource / scaffold
       ↓
Teacher approval
```

That full workflow appears much less well served.
